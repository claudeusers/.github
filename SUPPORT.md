# Support

This project is maintained by volunteers in their spare time. Here's the best way to get help, depending on what you need.

## I have a question or need help

Please use [GitHub Discussions](https://github.com/orgs/claudeusers/discussions) rather than opening an issue. Discussions are the right place for:

- "How do I...?" questions
- General feedback or ideas
- Conversation that isn't a specific bug or defect

## I found a bug or something is broken

Open an issue in the specific repository where the problem is, using the **Bug report** template. Please include:

- What you expected to happen
- What actually happened
- Steps to reproduce, if applicable

## I have a security concern

**Do not open a public issue.** See [SECURITY.md](./SECURITY.md) for how to report it privately.

## I have a question about Claude or Anthropic products directly

This organization is an unofficial community project and can't provide support for Anthropic's products themselves. Please see:

- [Anthropic Support](https://support.claude.com)
- [Claude Docs](https://docs.claude.com)
