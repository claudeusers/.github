# 👋 Welcome to Claude Users

**An unofficial, community-run hub for people who build with and use [Claude](https://claude.ai) — the AI models by [Anthropic](https://www.anthropic.com).**

---

## What is this?

Claude Users is a place for the community around Claude to gather, share, and build together — whether you're:

- 🧑‍💻 Building with the **Claude API**, **Claude Code**, or **MCP**
- 💬 A daily **claude.ai** or mobile app power user
- 🛠️ Sharing prompts, workflows, configs, or integrations
- 🌱 Looking to connect with other Claude users

## What you'll find here

- **Guides & resources** — prompts, workflows, and tips from the community
- **Showcase** — projects people have built with Claude
- **Discussions** — a place to ask questions, share wins, and swap ideas

*(Repos and links will be added here as they go live.)*
